# The numbers in the code

Generated by `tools/consts.py --doc` from the four executables, walked
from their entry points by `tools/rdis.py`. Do not edit it: the names and
the evidence live in `data/constants.json`, and everything else here is
counted out of the code.

555 distinct numbers over 11985 sites, and
296 distinct displacements off a register over 14906 sites.

A number's *role* is read off the instruction and its neighbours:

| role | what it means |
| --- | --- |
| argument | it lands in $a0..$a3 just before a call, and the call is named |
| mask | `andi` |
| bound | the `slti`/`sltiu` that limits an index |
| compared with | loaded, then tested by `beq`/`bne` -- a state or a kind |
| multiplier | built out of shifts and adds, e.g. 800 as `((x*3)<<3 + x)<<5` |
| divisor | an `sra` by 4..15 -- a scale, not a field being pulled out |
| added to | a displacement onto a pointer |
| loaded | none of the above: it is just put in a register |

Multipliers matter more than they look. `collide_at_cell` indexes the
terrain grid at `cz * 800 + cx * 10` and **neither number is in its
code** -- the compiler built both out of shifts and adds. An index of
immediates alone cannot see the two numbers that say what the grid is.
